Practical guidance

Payment information to discuss

1. Confirm the price in writing

Ask for the total amount for your requested dates and service, including what is included and any extra charges. Keep a copy of the quote or written response.

2. Verify payment instructions

Use contact details you trust to confirm any payment instructions. Do not rely on unexpected changes to payment details without checking them directly with the inn.

3. Understand deposits and balances

Ask whether a deposit is needed, when it is due, and when the remaining amount must be paid. Do not assume that terms from an earlier enquiry apply to a new booking.

4. Keep proof of payment

Save receipts, transaction references, and related messages. Send proof of payment to the inn only through a contact method it has confirmed.

5. Ask about refunds and changes

Before paying, clarify any applicable refund, cancellation, or date-change conditions. See the cancellation information page and booking terms.

6. Protect personal and financial information

Share only information needed for the transaction and avoid sending passwords, PINs, or one-time security codes to anyone.
