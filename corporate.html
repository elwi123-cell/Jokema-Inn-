Prepare your enquiry

Details worth including

A concise request can help avoid unnecessary back-and-forth. Include the information you already know; the team can advise if anything else is needed.

  • Requested arrival and departure dates
  • Number of guests and rooms required
  • Expected arrival time, especially for late arrivals
  • Whether the enquiry is for an individual or a company
  • Any room preferences or practical requirements
  • Who will make payment and receive the confirmation
  • Whether an invoice or company documentation is needed
  • A contact name and phone number or email for follow-up

Please avoid sending sensitive personal or payment-card information in an initial enquiry. Confirm payment procedures directly with Jokema Inn.

Helpful answers

Corporate travel questions

Are corporate rates available?

Corporate rates or negotiated arrangements are not listed here. Contact Jokema Inn with your dates and expected booking pattern to ask what options, if any, are available.

Can we enquire for several staff members?

You can contact the team with a group or multi-room request. Availability, room allocation and any group arrangements must be confirmed directly.

Can the inn provide an invoice or receipt?

Ask about available documentation and the details required for your organisation before finalising payment.

How do we confirm a booking?

Submit an enquiry and wait for direct confirmation from Jokema Inn. Sending an enquiry through this website does not itself guarantee a reservation.
