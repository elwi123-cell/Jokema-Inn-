Partners & collaboration

Building useful connections in Lae.

Local relationships can help organisations connect people with services, experiences and practical support. If your business or organisation has a proposal relevant to hospitality, guest services, events or the wider local community, contact Jokema Inn to introduce it.

This page describes the kinds of proposals you may enquire about. It does not indicate that any particular partnership, supplier agreement or referral programme is already in place.

Possible areas to discuss

What would you like to explore?

These are examples of collaboration topics—not confirmed services, commitments or existing partnerships.

01

Local suppliers

Introduce products or services that may be relevant to hospitality operations. Include your business details, product range and contact information.

02

Business and travel referrals

Discuss a potential referral or cross-promotion idea that could be useful to visitors and local organisations.

03

Events and group needs

If you support event organisers, teams or visiting groups, outline the type of collaboration you have in mind and the arrangements you propose.

04

Community initiatives

Share a community-focused idea, its intended benefit, the people involved and any support you are requesting.

05

Professional services

Businesses offering relevant professional or operational services can describe their experience and proposed scope of work.

06

Marketing collaboration

Suggest a promotion or content idea, including the audience, timing, responsibilities and how results could be assessed.

Make your proposal useful

What to include in your message

A short, well-organised introduction makes it easier to understand your idea and decide how to respond.

  • Your name, organisation and role
  • A reliable phone number and email address
  • A short description of your products or services
  • The partnership idea and expected benefit
  • Proposed dates, location or timeframe
  • What you would like Jokema Inn to contribute
  • Any relevant references, website or portfolio
  • Next steps you suggest for both parties
A straightforward process

From introduction to next steps

01 · Introduce yourself

Send your organisation’s details and a clear summary of your idea.

02 · Share the details

Provide any relevant proposal, timing, requirements and supporting information.

03 · Discuss suitability

If the proposal is of interest, discuss scope, responsibilities and practicalities.

04 · Confirm in writing

Do not assume a partnership is agreed until relevant terms are confirmed by authorised representatives.

Please note: Sending a partnership enquiry does not guarantee a response, approval, contract or commercial arrangement. Avoid sending confidential information or sensitive personal data in an initial message. Any collaboration should be assessed and agreed directly by the parties involved.
